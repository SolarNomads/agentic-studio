# GoFreeAlts: free alternatives, a curated video index and indexing checks

[Back to overview](../README.md)

## Problem
People looking for free or cheaper alternatives to paid AI and developer tools get stale lists. And a new site has a second problem: proving to search engines and to readers that it is fresh and crawlable.

## System built
- **Site:** static pages generated by Python from data files; prices are quoted with a checked date and a source; no third-party scripts or fonts. A "swap my stack" picker (state in the URL, share links with generated preview images) sums the saving from the checked prices only.
- **Watch index (gofreealts.com/watch):** the YouTube videos that builders recommend in Hacker News comments. Built from a public dataset export (credit and license shown on the page) plus our own pull of Hacker News comments since 2024, filtered for spam and tagged by topic. Machine-readable `data.json` (stable schema), per-topic JSON and `llms.txt`. Thumbnails are self-hosted so no third-party request happens until a video is opened.
- **Indexing tooling:** `index_check.py` (offline against the build, `--live` against the deployed site with a crawler user agent), and `indexnow.py` to notify search engines after each deploy.
- **Layout and browser tests** at phone and desktop widths.

## What exists and what does not (the "watcher")
There is no always-on monitoring service. What exists is the Watch index above, refreshed by data-pull scripts and rebuilt on demand, and a scheduled "watch of the day" social post that was defined on 1 Nov 2026 (Mon/Wed/Fri). I do not claim a live alerting watcher.

## Evidence
- Watch data (data.json, public, checked 10 Nov 2026): 738 videos. Build stats from 1 Nov 2026: 91,670 Hacker News items scanned, 61,226 comments with a YouTube link, 1,540 niche candidates, 1,814 reviewed by a model filter, 740 kept (413 from the public export, 327 from our own pull). [E7](../evidence/README.md#e7)
- Search Console, 2-8 Nov 2026: 6 clicks and 507 impressions (3 clicks and 123 impressions for 22-27 Oct). Small and early. [E5](../evidence/README.md#e5)
- Public pages return 200 (checked 10 Nov 2026); sitemap lists 24 URLs.
- Revenue: none yet.

## What I would do for a client
Give each data-driven page a "checked on" date and source, publish machine-readable versions next to the HTML, and run an indexing check before and after every deploy. Add a real monitor only when a decision depends on it.
